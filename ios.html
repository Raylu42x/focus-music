iOS does not let a web page set playback volume, so use the hardware buttons. Play it quieter than feels right — roughly 45–55 dBA. It should sit underneath your thoughts, not beside them.

WHY THIS PAGE EXISTS

01

iOS suspends Web Audio in the background. The full version generates its masking noise live and filters the output, which means an audio graph — and the moment you lock an iPhone or switch apps, iOS stops it. This page never creates one.

02

So the noise is baked in instead. Each of the seven levels is a separate mixed file. That keeps playback on the plain media path, which keeps running with the screen locked and shows up in your lock-screen controls.

03

Switching is seamless in position, not instant. All seven files are sample-aligned with each other, so your place in the music carries across exactly — but the new one has to buffer first, so expect a short pause when you move the slider. Pick a level and leave it.

04

The trade-off is warmth and the 40 Hz pulse. Both need live processing, so they only exist in the full version. The track is already mixed dark, so you are not missing much.

05

Everything else is the same music — beatless, no melody to follow, nothing modulating in the 4–8 Hz band where speech lives, and flat enough in loudness that nothing pulls your eyes off the screen. It loops forever with no seam.
